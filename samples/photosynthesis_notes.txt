Photosynthesis - Study Notes

Photosynthesis is the process plants use to turn light energy into chemical
energy. It takes place mainly in the chloroplasts of leaf cells, which contain
a green pigment called chlorophyll.

The process needs three inputs: carbon dioxide from the air, water from the
soil, and energy from sunlight. It produces glucose, which the plant uses for
energy and growth, and releases oxygen as a by-product.

Photosynthesis has two stages. The light-dependent reactions happen in the
thylakoid membranes and produce ATP and NADPH. The Calvin cycle happens in the
stroma and uses that ATP and NADPH to build glucose from carbon dioxide.
